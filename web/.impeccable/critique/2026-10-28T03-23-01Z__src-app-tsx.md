## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Authored for this product, not interchangeable with anything else. The 4px category stripe + tinted tag, cost chip per node, "~" on usage-based figures, the quantity × unit price × SKU line, a "Not priced (n)" section and the "[stack] vs [stack]" pickers are all specific to terraprev. The inspector and the Report fully deliver "The Engineering Drawing". The canvas does not, at first sight: the default fit is 0.32 scale at 1440×900 (0.15 on mobile), so 12px labels render near 4px. The landing view is a thumbnail of the drawing, and the side panel becomes what people read first.

**Deterministic scan:** `impeccable detect` found 21 findings, all in styles.css: 3 side-tab warnings (`.rnode` :238, `.doc__note` :584, `.finding` :632), 14 off-scale radii (mostly 4px, plus 2px and 10px), 4 off-ramp font sizes (16px at :664, :667, :724; 20px at :695). The in-page overlay found 119–265 hits per view, dominated by 10px text (`rnode__hint`, `gnode__sub`, `edge-label`, `lines__sku`), low contrast and tiny text. It confirmed and widened the contrast problem the review found: `lines__sku` is 2.6:1 in light, and category hues used as text in dark measure 3.2–3.6:1 (Database Blue, Network Violet, Security Rust, Integration Rose). The detector also caught report paragraphs at 112–116 characters per line, which the review missed.

False positives: side-tab on `.rnode` (documented Category-Stripe Rule), nested-cards on provider/VPC/subnet (diagram boundaries, not cards), cream-palette (documented Warm Paper), overused Inter (system choice), text-occlusion (caused by the detector's own labels), 16px slider input (documented iOS zoom guard). `.finding` and `.doc__note` stripes are defensible under DESIGN.md's "left stripes next to words".

**Visual overlays:** injected successfully; a [Human] tab in the browser pane shows the overlays on the Light-theme diagram view.

## Overall Impression

The trust layer is excellent: click a resource and every dollar shows its working, with honest unknowns. The two things holding it back are the landing view (a drawing you can't read) and accessibility (keyboard users can't operate the diagram, and muted text fails contrast). The single biggest opportunity: make the first view readable and bring findings into the live panel, so the right-after-apply check needs no Report.

## What's Working

1. **Every number shows its working.** The inspector's cost lines give quantity × unit price, free-tier notes, SKU and raw price-file link; "~" marks estimated figures. This is the product's core promise, implemented literally.
2. **Honest unknowns.** "Not priced (n)" with a reason per address, free resources counted, price publication dates, and an explicit list of what's excluded.
3. **Two prints of one drawing.** Light and dark are real re-paperings (warm night paper, no neon), the category stripe and single accent hold in both, and the Report reads as a real document.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Keyboard users can't operate the diagram, and focus is invisible**
- Why it matters: WCAG 2.2 AA is a stated requirement (2.1.1, 2.4.7, 2.4.11, 2.4.3), and the diagram is the product. 70 tab stops; 9 focusable edges with `outline: none`; Enter on a focused node does nothing because only `onNodeClick` selects (Diagram.tsx:149); canvas tools come at stop 56 with no skip link.
- Fix: `edgesFocusable={false}`; select on Enter/Space for focused nodes; a 2px accent focus ring on `.react-flow__node:focus-visible .rnode` that stays legible at low zoom (or zoom to the focused node); move `.canvas__tools` before the canvas in DOM order; add "Skip to panel".
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

**[P1] The default diagram fit is unreadable**
- Why it matters: breaks "understand the shape in under a minute"; the signature component is invisible on landing; selecting a node doesn't bring it into view.
- Fix: fit with a minimum readable zoom (≥0.6) and pan to the VPC, the most expensive node, or what just changed; zoom to selection when the inspector opens; on ≤900px start at a readable zoom.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout (then /impeccable adapt for mobile)

**[P1] Findings are missing from the live app**
- Why it matters: the Report lists 2 high and 5 medium findings (postgres backups off, unencrypted storage, instances in public subnets), but the live panel has only Cost / Usage / Resource tabs (App.tsx:337) and nodes carry no marker. An engineer checking right after apply never sees them.
- Fix: a "Findings (7)" tab or a top section with severity pill + text, click-through to the node; a small severity stamp on affected nodes.
- Suggested command: /impeccable shape (then /impeccable clarify)

**[P1] Muted, warn, SKU and category-label text fail AA contrast**
- Why it matters: WCAG 1.4.3, and this is the text that carries the "show the working" caveats. `--ink-3` is 3.6:1 on panel in light and 4.0:1 in dark; `kpi__label` 3.2:1; `lines__sku` 2.6:1; `--warn` 4.3:1; `sev--low/good` ~3.4:1; category hues as text in dark 3.2–3.6:1. DESIGN.md claims ink-3 is "never below WCAG AA at its size", which is false.
- Fix: darken light ink-3 to ~#6e7076 and warn to ~#a2581a; lift dark ink-3; give category-colored text a lighter dark-theme variant (keep stripes as they are); recheck pills; correct DESIGN.md.
- Suggested command: /impeccable colorize (then /impeccable audit)

**[P2] Dialog, listbox and tab semantics**
- Why it matters: screen-reader and keyboard users lose their place. The Report is `role="dialog"` without `aria-modal`, focus doesn't move in or return, Tab reaches hidden buttons (ReportView.tsx:149). The stack-picker listbox has no `aria-activedescendant`. Tabs have no tabpanels or arrow keys.
- Fix: `<dialog>.showModal()` or aria-modal + focus trap + focus return; option ids + aria-activedescendant; real tabpanels with arrow-key roving, or drop the tab roles.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** one shortcut (`\`); no "/" jump-to-resource; no keyboard open for nodes; zooms the map on every visit; selection doesn't center; 25 usage sliders with no filter; number fields show "1000000" next to "≈ 1M"; serial hidden by truncation.

**Sam (keyboard + screen reader):** invisible focus on 9 edge stops; Enter on a node does nothing; Report dialog doesn't take or trap focus; listbox options not announced; muted and warn text fail contrast; live dot is color-only (App.tsx:180); apply pulse and banner fade ignore reduced motion (only path edges are covered, styles.css:520); theme buttons announce "☀ Light" / "☾ Dark".

**Priya (platform engineer checking a plan before merge):** no empty-state hint when no plans exist ("Run terraform plan -out=… to preview here"); destroys aren't ordered first or escalated, so a database destroy looks like a tag change; a cost jump has sign and color but no proportion cue; existing high findings are invisible live, so she can't tell if the plan fixes them; no "Copy PR comment" in the live plan view.

## Questions to Consider

1. If the inspector is where trust happens, should the landing view open on the change that just applied (or the most expensive resource) instead of a map nobody can read?
2. Findings are the most actionable output, yet the live app hides them behind "Report". Is terraprev a cost tool that draws, or an architecture review that prices? The panel's tab order should say which.
3. In plan mode, should destroying a stateful resource (RDS, DynamoDB, S3) break the "nothing shouting" rule on purpose?
